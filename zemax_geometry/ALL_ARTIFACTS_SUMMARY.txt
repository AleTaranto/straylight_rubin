READ THIS FIRST. The question behind this analysis was whether each artifact
type always has a bright star at some specific off-axis distance from the
boresight. The short answer is that the naive way of asking -- compare the
artifact rate inside a candidate annulus with the rate outside -- gives badly
misleading answers here, and most of the apparently strong results it produces
are not real.

The reason is burstiness. Artifact labels arrive in runs of consecutive
exposures on a handful of nights, and the survey revisits the same fields, so
whichever annulus that night's field happens to populate will look enriched.
Measured against a null that reproduces the burst structure, the largest
'lift' the scan finds by chance alone is already between 1.5x and 5.8x
depending on the artifact. So a 4x enrichment is not evidence of anything --
it is the typical value of the null.

WHAT THE TELEMETRY CANNOT SETTLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Which surface scatters the light. A feature fixed in the camera frame
implicates the optics, one fixed in the dome frame the structure, one fixed on
the sky the atmosphere. Separating those needs the source direction measured
in each frame, and this dataset provides only about one bright star per
exposure inside any candidate annulus. With a single direction the three
frames differ by fixed offsets, and two stars at the same radius rotate
together, so no amount of additional telemetry of this kind breaks the
degeneracy. Answering it needs the artifact's own orientation measured in the
pixels.

Label completeness. Artifacts are found by human inspection, so every rate
here is a rate of labelling. The burst structure is consistent with real
events and also with inspection attention.

What the artifact names mean physically. Apart from 'horseshoe' (L3 lens
baffle reflection, documented in L3_baffle/horseshoe_detailer.py) and 'tape'
(scattered light, test LVV-T956-a), no physical definition of these names is
recorded anywhere in this repository. The causes named above are inferred from
geometry alone.
